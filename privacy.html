IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Privacy & your enquiries.

Brisbane Landscaping Group respects your privacy. This page explains what happens when you contact us through this website.

Information you choose to share

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, suburb, budget, and details or photos of your project. We use this information to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote and communicate about services you request.

How the enquiry form works

The form on this website prepares an email in your own email application. It does not submit or store your enquiry on this website. You must press Send in your email application to deliver it. Your email provider and ours will then process your message under their respective privacy terms.

Third-party services

This site may load fonts from Google Fonts and illustrative images from Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when their resources are loaded. The website itself does not run advertising tracking scripts or set analytics cookies.

Contact us

To ask about information you have sent us, call 0423 981 128 or use the email link. We will handle reasonable correction or deletion requests, subject to records we must retain.
