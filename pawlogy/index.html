A thoughtful training plan

What does progress look like?

  1. Understand the triggers. We learn when your dog becomes distressed and what helps them settle.
  2. Build a secure routine. We begin with short, manageable separations and calm, positive associations.
  3. Practise at your dog's pace. Time apart grows gradually, without forcing a dog through panic.
  4. Transfer the skills home. We guide your family to keep practising between sessions.

Some families plan a programme of around 10 sessions. Every dog is different: the number of sessions and outcome cannot be guaranteed. Severe cases may benefit from veterinary behavioural advice.

Helpful answers

Questions?
We've got you.

Every journey starts with a conversation about your dog.

Do you work with puppies and adult dogs?

Yes. Enquire about puppy foundations, adult-dog skills and tailored training support. We'll discuss what suits your dog's age and needs.

Can you help with barking or lead pulling?

We can discuss common training and behavioural concerns. For cases that may need veterinary or clinical behavioural care, we'll recommend appropriate professional assessment.

Can you help my puppy learn to stay home alone?

Yes. We help with gradual independence and separation-related concerns through carefully paced sessions and owner practice at home. Progress varies, and no fixed number of lessons guarantees a result.

How many sessions will we need?

It depends on your dog's age, history and level of distress. A programme of around 10 sessions can be a starting point for planning, but we assess each dog individually and adapt as we go.

Can I book dog sitting for several days or longer?

Yes, short and extended dog sitting arrangements can be discussed, subject to availability, your dog's care requirements and an agreed quote.

Do you come to my suburb?

We take enquiries across Brisbane, Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast. Contact us with your suburb to confirm current availability and any travel fee.

How does booking work?

Complete the enquiry form below. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send; you can also call us directly. We confirm all details and pricing before a booking.
