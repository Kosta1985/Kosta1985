GOOD TO KNOW / 07

A few things
you might ask.

Still have questions? We're happy to talk through your project before anything is booked.

Ask us a question
Do you install new lawns and repair existing grass? +

Yes. We can prepare the ground and work with natural turf, grass seeding and repairs to damaged or patchy areas. We can also discuss artificial turf as a separate scope, which requires different base preparation.

Can you work with a smaller budget? +

Absolutely. We can look at different materials, plant selections and staged works so you can prioritise the parts that matter most.

Do you offer one-off and regular garden care? +

Yes. We can quote for one-off tidy-ups, mowing, edging, pruning and garden refreshes, or discuss repeat maintenance depending on your location.

Do you take away garden waste? +

Green-waste removal can be included in your quote. We will confirm what is being taken away so the scope and price are clear.

Do you charge for an on-site inspection? +

Some projects need a site visit and may involve a call-out fee. We'll always confirm any fee in advance, along with whether it can be credited towards your approved works.

What should I send to get started? +

Your suburb, a few photos, rough dimensions if you have them, and a short description of your idea. A budget range is helpful but optional.
