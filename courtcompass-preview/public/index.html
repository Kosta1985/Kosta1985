Self-representation preparation platform for Australian courts, tribunals and commissions. This preview contains no live legal backend, no document upload and no payment processing.

Commonwealth, Queensland and progressively every Australian state and territory.

2 · Build the matter

3 · Verify before publishing

Official source snapshots, effective-date checks, exact evidence quotations and citation-entailment review.

Safety architecture

Source-first

Current-law answers use managed official-source snapshots rather than model memory.

Privacy-first

Sensitive AI payload logging/cache is disabled; document handling is designed to require malware scanning.

Human escalation

High-risk matters and uncertain legal propositions can be routed to Australian lawyer review.
